NoteVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

NoteVault (“the app”) is a private notes app. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. NoteVault has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer (Ritesh Jha) never receives your notes or any other data from the app.

Where your notes are stored

Your notes are stored on your device, in the device’s secure storage (the iOS Keychain). The app does not send them to the developer or to any server. If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup for your device, your notes may be included in that backup by iOS. Those backups are managed by Apple and are subject to Apple’s privacy policy.

Locking the app

NoteVault asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode before it opens. This is handled by your device’s operating system. The app does not receive or store your biometric data.

Backups and exports that you create

  • Encrypted backup: if you choose this option, the app creates a file containing your notes, encrypted with a password that you choose. You decide where to save it, for example iCloud Drive. We cannot read this file and cannot recover your password.
  • Plain text export: if you choose this option, the app creates an unencrypted text file of your notes and you decide where to save it.

Once you save a file to a service such as iCloud Drive, that service’s own privacy policy applies to it.

Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone, because we do not have it.

Children

NoteVault is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy and the date above.
